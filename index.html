Hermes_Personal

Hermes_Personal is a privately operated Google account connector for its owner's Hermes assistant. It connects accounts that the owner explicitly authorizes, using Google's OAuth consent flow.

The connector supports email and calendar work. Existing accounts may also retain their separately authorized Drive, Docs, Sheets and read-only Contacts access.

This is a personal connector. It is not a public hosted service and does not offer public registration.
