Privacy information

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Purpose and access

This connector is operated for its owner's personal assistant. Google account identity, email, calendar and other explicitly authorized Workspace data are accessed to carry out the owner's requests and configured workflows. The Google consent screen lists the permissions requested for each account.

Processing and storage

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's controlled host. Assistant history, workflow state and operational logs may retain information used during a task. Relevant content can be sent to the AI providers configured for the assistant when an authorized task or workflow requires model processing. Google also processes API requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

These public documentation pages do not display connected account content or credentials.

Control and retention

The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase information already retained in assistant history, logs or backups. The operator controls those records and their deletion.

Contact

For questions about this personal connector, contact the operator through mwarevito's GitHub profile.
